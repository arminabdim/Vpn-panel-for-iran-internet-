OpenPath is a tool for access to information. It contains no tracking, calls no third-party service, and stores nothing outside your own device and browser.

How you use it is your responsibility — follow the laws and stay safe. Public proxy configs are operated by strangers: never send passwords or bank details through a node you did not create yourself.
